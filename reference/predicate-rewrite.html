Date / time periods

Avoid (column wrapped)Prefer (range on column)
YEAR(d) = 2024 d >= DATE '2024-01-01' AND d < DATE '2025-01-01'
DATE(d) = '2024-06-01' d >= … AND d < … + 1 day
TO_CHAR(d, 'YYYY-MM') = '2024-06' d >= DATE '2024-06-01' AND d < DATE '2024-07-01'
TRUNC(d) = TRUNC(sysdate) d >= day_start AND d < day_end
